Privacy Policy for Golden Hour

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Golden Hour mobile application ("Golden Hour," "the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

Golden Hour does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other personal identifiers.

Location Data. The App uses your device's location (or a location you manually enter) solely to calculate golden hour, blue hour, sunrise, and sunset times for your area. This location data is used to provide the App's core functionality and is not linked to your identity.

We do not collect, and the App does not contain, any analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking software development kits (SDKs).

How We Use Information

Location data is used only to:

  • Calculate accurate golden hour, blue hour, sunrise, and sunset times based on where you are (or a location you specify)
  • Provide time zone–accurate results for that location

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the App's core functionality.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your location or any other information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.

Data Storage and Retention

Where location data is processed only on your device to display golden hour times, it is not transmitted to or stored on our servers. If any location processing occurs via a remote service to compute results, that data is used transiently for the calculation and is not retained or associated with you afterward.

Children's Privacy

Golden Hour is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Your Choices

You can control the App's access to your device's location at any time through your device's Settings. If you deny or revoke location access, you can still use the App by manually entering a location, though some convenience features may be limited.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
